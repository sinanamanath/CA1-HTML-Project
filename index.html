About SkillBridge

Learn any skill. Teach what you know.

SkillBridge is a web-based peer tutoring and skill exchange platform that connects students who can teach a subject or skill with students who want to learn it. Whether you are strong in Calculus, Python, Photography or Spanish, there is someone on campus who can learn from you, and someone you can learn from.

Our mission: to encourage peer-to-peer learning and create a structured environment where students can exchange knowledge and skills, completely free.

How it works

1

Create your profile

Sign up and tell us who you are.

2

List your skills

Add what you can teach, what you want to learn, and when you are available.

3

Find your match

Search and filter to find suitable peers.

4

Start learning

Send a request, schedule a session and rate your experience.

What you can do on SkillBridge

  • Profile management
  • Skill listing
  • Search and filtering
  • Peer matching
  • Session requests
  • Scheduling
  • Notifications
  • Ratings

Why peer learning?

💬

Easy to understand

Classmates often explain things in the way you understand best.

🧠

Teach to learn

🤝

Free and friendly

No fees, just a helpful campus community.
